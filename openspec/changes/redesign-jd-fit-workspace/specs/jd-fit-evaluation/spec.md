# Spec Delta

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Citation context labels
Every citation SHALL carry `experience_title` (nullable), `experience_type`, and `evidence_kinds` taken from the cited revision's retained approved structured content, matched by the citation's logical experience and evidence identifiers. These labels SHALL NOT be generated by the model and SHALL NOT replace the cited source span or quote.

#### Scenario: Project evidence is cited
- **WHEN** a citation refers to evidence of kind `contribution` inside an approved project titled "Project Atlas"
- **THEN** the citation reports `experience_title: "Project Atlas"`, `experience_type: "project"`, and `evidence_kinds: ["contribution"]` alongside its unchanged quote and source span

#### Scenario: General experience without a title
- **WHEN** a citation refers to general experience whose approved title is null
- **THEN** the citation reports `experience_title: null` and `experience_type: "general-experience"` without inventing a title

#### Scenario: One chunk covers several evidence items
- **WHEN** a cited chunk is linked to evidence items of different kinds
- **THEN** `evidence_kinds` lists each distinct kind once, in the order the evidence items appear in the approved revision

#### Scenario: Active revision differs from latest approved
- **WHEN** R1 is active and indexed while R2 is approved but not published
- **THEN** labels on R1 citations come from R1's approved content, never from R2's

#### Scenario: Labels cannot be resolved
- **WHEN** the cited revision's approved content cannot be read or does not contain the cited logical identifiers
- **THEN** the citation reports `experience_title: null` and an empty `evidence_kinds`, the report is still returned, and no label is generated

#### Scenario: Label lookup runs
- **WHEN** the system resolves citation labels
- **THEN** it performs read-only lookups of the already-retrieved revisions and does not widen retrieval or mutate ingestion state
